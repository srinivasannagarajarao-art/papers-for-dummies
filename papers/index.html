Papers

No proofs. No summations you have to squint at. Just the idea, translated into something you could set a breakpoint in.

How to read these: don't. Not first, anyway. Run the code, break it the way each page tells you to, then come back and read. Watching something fail teaches faster than watching someone explain.

ai-ml/transformers/

  • Byte-Pair Encoding: the tokeniser you inherited Sennrich et al., 2015

    Train BPE in a for loop, then watch why the model can't count letters and why the same sentence costs several times more in Tamil.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Attention Is All You Need Vaswani et al., 2017

    Attention is a dictionary lookup with fuzzy keys — everything else is plumbing. Runnable NumPy, four demos that prove it by breaking it.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • RoFormer: Rotary Position Embedding Su et al., 2021

    Rotate q and k instead of adding a position vector, and the dot product only sees distance. Every claim is a printed number.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • BERT: Deep Bidirectional Transformers Devlin et al., 2018

    Remove the causal mask, hide 15% of the tokens, guess them. Then bolt a head on per task. A tiny masked model, trained live.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • GPT: Generative Pre-trained Transformers Radford et al., 2018 to Brown et al., 2020

    Decoder-only, next token, then scale. The generation loop is a while loop. A character-level GPT trained in your terminal.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • An Image Is Worth 16×16 Words (ViT) Dosovitskiy et al., 2020

    The whole paper is a reshape: cut the image into patches and it's a sentence. Shapes printed at every step.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Models Touvron et al., 2023

    Not a new idea, a recipe: four deltas on the 2017 transformer and far more tokens than the scaling laws said. Parameter arithmetic included.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

ai-ml/scaling/

  • Chinchilla: how big a model should you train? Hoffmann et al., 2022

    Fixed compute budget: spend it on a bigger model or more data? Roughly twenty tokens per parameter, swept and plotted.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Mixture of Experts: the Sparsely-Gated Layer Shazeer et al., 2017

    A load balancer in front of eight small networks. Eight times the parameters, twice the compute. Hot shards and dropped tokens, as in production.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • DeepSeek-V3: the recipe, and the interconnect that shaped it DeepSeek-AI, 2024

    What you build when your GPUs have the compute but not the bandwidth. Every choice traces to one halved interconnect.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

ai-ml/inference/

  • The GPU, for people who only ever call .cuda() The hardware every other page assumes

    One number, arithmetic intensity, predicts which of your operations will be slow before you profile anything.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • KV caching and the generation loop Folklore, and the papers that fixed it

    Why inference is affordable. The causal mask makes keys and values immutable, so you memoise them. Includes the cache-size arithmetic that decides what you can serve.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Grouped-Query Attention: one set of keys for several heads Ainslie et al., 2023

    The default in every modern open model. One repeat operation, and getting it wrong produces plausible garbage with no error.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Multi-head Latent Attention: the KV cache, compressed DeepSeek-AI, 2024

    Cache one small latent instead of every key and value, and fold the decompression into the query projection so it costs nothing.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • FlashAttention: attention was never compute-bound Dao et al., 2022

    The n² cost is memory traffic, not arithmetic. Tile it, keep a running softmax, never store the score matrix.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Quantisation: what fits on your GPU Dettmers et al., 2022, and what followed

    Divide, round, store. LLM.int8, GPTQ, AWQ and QLoRA's NF4 are four arguments about which numbers you may be sloppy with.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Speculative decoding: guess, then check Leviathan et al., 2022

    A cheap model guesses ahead, the big one verifies in a single pass. Branch prediction for tokens, with the output provably unchanged.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • How a serving engine actually works Kwon et al., 2023, and what surrounds it

    Continuous batching and paged memory. Your inference server is an operating system, and this is its scheduler and allocator.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

ai-ml/fine-tuning/

  • LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation Hu et al., 2021

    A fine-tune is a diff on the weights, and the diff is low-rank. Store the patch, not the repo. Merge it for free at inference.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning: the family Houlsby et al., 2019, and what followed

    One question five ways: where do you insert the small trainable thing? Adapters, prefixes, prompts, (IA)³ and LoRA, with parameter counts.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

ai-ml/alignment/

  • Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback Christiano et al., 2017

    You can't write the loss for "helpful", but you can ask which of two answers is better. Reward hacking, reproduced on purpose.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • InstructGPT: Following Instructions with Human Feedback Ouyang et al., 2022

    The three-stage recipe that turned a completion engine into an assistant. Each stage's gain in one table. This is ChatGPT's recipe.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Direct Preference Optimization Rafailov et al., 2023

    The RLHF objective has an exact answer, and rearranging it makes the reward model disappear. Preference tuning becomes one classifier loss.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • GRPO: reinforcement learning without a critic Shao et al., 2024

    PPO needs a second model to guess the baseline. Sample a group of answers and use their own mean instead. The algorithm behind R1.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • DeepSeek-R1: reasoning nobody taught it DeepSeek-AI, 2025

    Reward only correct answers and long reasoning appears on its own, because thinking longer was the cheapest way to be right.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

ai-ml/generative/

  • Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (VAE) Kingma and Welling, 2013

    An autoencoder whose bottleneck is a distribution. The reparameterisation trick is moving the random number outside the function.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Generative Adversarial Nets Goodfellow et al., 2014

    A forger and a detective train each other. Mode collapse and vanishing gradients, triggered deliberately on a toy problem.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models Ho et al., 2020

    Learn to undo a noise-adding for-loop one step at a time. The forward process is a formula; the model is a one-step undo.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

ai-ml/retrieval/

  • Sentence-BERT and CLIP: making distance mean something Reimers & Gurevych, 2019 · Radford et al., 2021

    Where embeddings come from. A model trained for something else gives a useless distance, measured and then fixed with contrastive loss.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation Lewis et al., 2020

    An open-book exam. The retriever is the index, the prompt is the page you slide across. The failures are retrieval bugs, not model bugs.

    English தமிழ் हिंदी
